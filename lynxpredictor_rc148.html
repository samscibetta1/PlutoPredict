              What PlutoPredict is

              A prediction engine for Phase 2 oncology assets — scored at the moment the first efficacy readout exists and the pivotal commitment has not yet been made.

              The decision it is built for

              Phase 2 is where the evidence is thinnest and the money is largest. A single-arm ORR in thirty patients is the only efficacy signal, the pivotal design is not fixed, and the licensing market prices the asset anyway. The engine takes that same public evidence, scores it, and produces a falsifiable number — a probability of approval, a launch date, and a revenue curve — that can be checked afterwards against what happened and against what the market paid.

              The unit of analysis

              The asset–indication–date triple, never the asset alone. An asset is not one prediction but several: each indication carries its own stage, its own base rate, its own competitive field and its own patient pool. Fixing the prediction date matters as much — the engine may only see evidence published on or before it, which is what makes a backtest possible.

              How it runs

              Four stages from raw public data to a scored prediction.

              STAGE 01

              Ingest

              Seventeen public and licensed sources, normalised to the asset–indication grain and cut off at the prediction date.

              STAGE 02

              Resolve

              Trials, publications, filings and claims are linked to a single asset identity, and indications mapped to a common taxonomy. The Asset Selection tab is this stage made visible.

              STAGE 03

              Score features

              Raw attributes collapse into five feature families on a 0–100 scale. These are the inputs both predictors share.

              STAGE 04

              Predict

              The approval predictor outputs probability and timing. The commercial predictor turns those into a risk-adjusted revenue curve.

              The five feature families

              Everything ingested resolves into these. The approval predictor uses the first four; the commercial predictor uses Market Addressability and Competitive Intensity.

              Risk-adjusted NPV

              The predicted current value of the asset itself, independent of any deal that has or has not been signed. Every indication's revenue curve is weighted by its probability of approval, ramped to peak share from its own predicted launch, discounted back to the prediction date, and netted for the development still to be paid for.

              From unrisked revenue to asset NPV

              Where the value goes: approval risk first, then operating margin, then the development still to be funded.
